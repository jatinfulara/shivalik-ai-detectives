About this lab

Shivalik AI Detectives is a 60-minute interactive workshop for Classes 6–11, part of PROJECT UMEED by the Shivalik Children Science Foundation. It works offline in your browser, needs no login, and saves progress only on this device. Nothing you type or choose is uploaded.

Honest labels: the “Train your own AI” mission is a simplified classroom demo, not a real neural network. The courtroom cases are fictional. Real machine learning is available at Google Teachable Machine, which opens separately.

Presented by Jatin Fulara, Regional Outreach Coordinator, Shivalik Children Science Foundation.
